Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Videl is built so your files stay on your device. This page explains exactly what leaves it, and when.

Your files

  • The desktop and Android apps process your files on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • The web app at app.videl.app processes your files inside your browser. They are never uploaded either.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

When the apps go online

The desktop and Android apps only connect to the internet to:

  • Check for and download updates, from GitHub.
  • Install optional AI components, only when you choose to, from GitHub, PyPI (Python packages) and Hugging Face (AI models).
  • Download media from the links you give the downloader, directly from those sites. For a Spotify link, the app reads Spotify's public page for the track names and finds the songs on YouTube.
  • Send a bug report, when you press Send (see below).
  • Handle a Videl Pro license, in the desktop app, only when you use its License page or have a license (see below).

Like any download, these services see your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. The Videl browser extension, and the web app's Downloader page when you press Open in Videl, only talk to the Videl app on your own computer.

Videl Pro licenses

The free tools never contact our license server. The desktop app talks to it, at license.videl.app on Cloudflare, only when you press Start 7-day trial, Buy Pro (which also fetches the current prices) or I have a license key on its License page, and once you have a Videl Pro license or trial.

What the app sends

  • A device ID with every license request. It is an 8-character code made from a one-way scramble (a hash) of your computer's system ID. It can't be turned back into that ID, and it ties your license to this computer.
  • The app version, with every request.
  • When you buy: the plan, the payment method, and the name and email or phone number you enter. Later, the transaction reference you type in.
  • At check-in, at most once a day while you're online: your license key and device ID.

The app never sends anything about your files or which tools you use. The count of free daily runs stays on your computer.

What the server stores

  • Your license: its key, plan, end date, and the name and contact you entered.
  • Your orders: the order code, plan, amount, payment method, name, contact, transaction reference and status.
  • The device IDs using your license, with when each first and last checked in.
  • The device IDs that have had a free trial, so each computer gets one trial.
  • A log of what we did on your license, such as approving a payment or moving a device.

Your IP address is used only to limit how many requests can be sent per minute. It isn't stored. When you send a transaction reference, we get an email at the support inbox with the order code, plan, payment method and reference, so we can check the payment in our own wallet or bank app.

Payments

You pay through InstaPay or your mobile wallet, never inside Videl. Their own privacy policies apply, and we see what any payment shows us: the amount, the reference, and the sender's name or number.

On your computer

The license file holds your license key, plan, name, device ID and dates. Your contact details are never put in it.

How long records are kept

We keep license and order records while your license is in use, and afterwards as a record of payment. To have your name and contact details removed, email videl.support@gmail.com with your license key or order code.

The websites

  • videl.app and app.videl.app are hosted by Cloudflare, which handles connection data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the web app's video engine (FFmpeg) loads from jsDelivr. Your browser contacts them directly when it fetches these files.
  • The download page asks GitHub for the latest version number, and the installers download from GitHub.
  • The web app remembers how often you use each tool, in your browser's own storage, so your most used tools come first. This never leaves your device. Clearing the site's data removes it.

Bug reports

What is sent

Only when you press Send in the bug reporter:

  • The type, title and description you write, and a screenshot if you add one.
  • Your email address, only if you enter it.
  • Where the report came from: the app version and operating system, or your browser's name and version in the web app.

Where it goes

The report travels through our relay on Cloudflare to the Videl support inbox, a Gmail account. Your IP address is used only to limit how many reports can be sent per minute. It isn't stored with the report.

If you enter your email

We send you one short confirmation with a reference number, through Resend, an email delivery service, and we may reply to you from the support inbox. To send at most one confirmation a day per address, a scrambled one-way form of your address (a hash) is kept for 24 hours and then deleted automatically.

How long reports are kept

Reports stay in the support inbox for as long as they help us fix problems. To have yours deleted, email videl.support@gmail.com with its reference number (it looks like VB-7K2Q9D).

Contact

Questions about this page or your data: videl.support@gmail.com. If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
