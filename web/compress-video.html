Compress video

Make a video smaller to fit email, WhatsApp or Discord limits. Pick the strength and resolution. Free, no sign-up, and your video never leaves your device.

How to compress a video

  1. Add your video. MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and most other formats work.
  2. Choose a strength. A lower resolution or removing the sound saves even more.
  3. Save the smaller MP4.

Questions

How much smaller will my video get?

It depends on the video. Stronger compression and a lower resolution give the biggest savings, and the result is an MP4 that plays everywhere.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how often you use it.

Are my files uploaded?

No. FFmpeg, the video engine behind most video software, runs inside your browser tab, so your files stay on your device.
