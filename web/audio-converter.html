Questions

Can I convert FLAC to MP3?

Yes. FLAC, Opus and most other audio files open, and you can save them as MP3, M4A, WAV or OGG.

Which format should I pick?

MP3 plays everywhere. M4A is a little smaller at the same quality. WAV keeps every detail but is large.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark. You get 10 free jobs a day across all the tools, and a batch of up to 10 files counts as one. Videl Pro removes the limit.

Are my files uploaded?

No. FFmpeg, the video engine behind most video software, runs inside your browser tab, so your files stay on your device.
